06 / Campaigns

Campaigns done.

Campaign work from the original portfolio, kept in its original wording.

December

The campaign was inspired by the hospital's logo, where each dot represents a core pillar of the institution's commitment to healthcare excellence.

Through a series of social media creatives, we explored the significance behind these dots and connected them to the values that define the hospital compassion, innovation, expertise, patient care, research, and excellence.

June - 3 campaigns

1. Mumbai - The Beauty Of Firsts, Protected By Us - This campaign focused on some of life's most meaningful milestones and how healthcare plays a role in making them possible.

The idea was built around the concept of "firsts" moments that people cherish and remember forever.

From a newborn's first cry to a stroke survivor's first word, a patient's first meal after surgery, or their first walk after a knee replacement, every milestone represents hope, recovery, and a new beginning.

2. Navi Mumbai - Because in a crisis, the bridge is too far - This campaign was built around a simple but powerful insight: during a medical emergency, distance matters.

For residents of Navi Mumbai, reaching Mumbai during peak traffic can be challenging when every minute counts.

The campaign highlighted how Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital, Navi Mumbai brings advanced healthcare closer to patients without requiring them to travel across the city.

The communication focused on showcasing world-class infrastructure, advanced technology, expert doctors, and comprehensive care available within Navi Mumbai itself.

3. Indore - वही भरोसेका हाथ। अब इंदौर के साथ। This campaign was designed to introduce Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital's presence in Indore while carrying forward the trust associated with the Mumbai institution.

The central idea was simple: the same expertise, trust, and commitment to patient care that people associate with Mumbai is now available in Indore.

A key highlight of the campaign was communicating that patients in Indore now have access to consultations from experienced doctors associated with Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital, Mumbai.
